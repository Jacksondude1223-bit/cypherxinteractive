Live Mythic combat

SMFT: Gods

A combat arena built on a simple promise: every god should feel completely different to play, and completely readable to fight. Pick a pantheon, learn its kit, and climb.

Under the hood it's a latency-tolerant combat model with server-side hit validation — fights resolve fairly whether you're on fibre or a phone on mobile data.

  • Distinct divine kits — each god has its own movement, resource and combo identity.
  • Ranked progression — seasonal ladders with rewards that carry between seasons.
  • Published balance notes — every change is documented before it goes live.
  • AXIOM protected — reach, speed and damage exploits are rejected server-side.

Live Tycoon

PlushX Tycoon

Start with one stitching bench. Finish with a fully automated plush factory. PlushX is a tycoon built around a real economy — production lines that actually feed each other, and upgrades that change how you play rather than just how fast you earn.

Progress is server-owned and versioned, so saves survive updates and nothing is lost to a bad session.

  • Layered automation — each tier unlocks a genuinely new production stage.
  • Tuned economy — curves modelled and tested so late game stays worth playing.
  • Collectible plushes — limited runs and event drops with permanent value.
  • Safe saves — versioned data with migrations, backed by Spheres infrastructure.
