After you hit send

Where your message actually goes.

Into a Discord channel the team watches, tagged by topic. There's no ticketing system and no autoresponder, which is why a reply reads like a person wrote it.

Arrives Posted to the studio channel within seconds of you sending it.
Triaged Picked up by whoever owns that topic, usually the same day.
Answered A real reply, or a note explaining what we're still checking.

Things worth knowing

  • We don't take on client work for hire, so those enquiries get a polite no fairly quickly.
  • Appeals sent from a second account get ignored, and the account gets banned as well.
  • Found an exploit? Send it here rather than posting it. We've rewarded people for that.
  • Lost progress needs reporting within seven days, before the backup rotates out.
  • We read everything. We don't always reply to "hi" on its own, though.
