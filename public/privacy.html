Privacy notice

How Spheres Hosting LLC handles personal information collected through the CypherX Interactive website and our Roblox experiences.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Template notice. This page is a starting point written for the CypherX Interactive site. Have it reviewed by a qualified legal adviser and adjusted to your actual data practices before you rely on it.

Who we are

CypherX Interactive is a brand of Spheres Hosting LLC ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for information collected through this website and our Roblox experiences. Contact us at privacy@cypherxinteractive.com.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us — name, email address and the content of any message you send through the contact form or by email.
  • In-experience data — Roblox user ID, progression and gameplay state needed to save your progress and operate our games.
  • Security telemetry — gameplay events processed by AXIOM Anticheat to detect exploitation, together with any resulting enforcement record.
  • Technical data — standard server and error logs generated when you use the site or our services.

Why we use it

  • To answer enquiries you send us.
  • To operate our games, save progress and provide player support.
  • To detect, investigate and act on cheating, fraud and abuse.
  • To keep our services secure, available and working correctly.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not embed third-party analytics. If that changes, this notice will be updated before any new tracking is enabled.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who host or support our systems, and where we are legally required to do so. Gameplay on Roblox is also subject to Roblox Corporation's own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Retention

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to handle them and for a reasonable period afterwards. Gameplay and security records are kept while your account is active with us and for as long as needed to enforce our terms and handle appeals.

Children

Our games are played on Roblox, which has a substantial young audience. We collect the minimum data required to run the experience, and we do not knowingly request personal details from children beyond what Roblox itself provides.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Write to privacy@cypherxinteractive.com and we will respond within the timeframe the law requires.

Changes

We will post any changes to this notice on this page and update the date above.
